FREE HOSTING (GitHub Pages)
1. Make a free account at github.com and click New repository (name it e.g. welcomehome, set Public).
2. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in EVERYTHING from this folder (index.html, ar.mp4, edit.mp4, target.jpg), then Commit.
3. Settings > Pages > Source: Deploy from a branch > main / (root) > Save.
4. After ~1 minute your site is at https://YOURNAME.github.io/welcomehome/
5. Open it on your iPhone (Safari), tap Start, allow camera, point it at a printed/on-screen copy of target.jpg.
